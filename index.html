    What this checks Rules, sources and assumptions

    14 CFR Part 117 (federal)

    • §117.13 Table B: max FDP by report time (local time where the FDP starts) and number of operated segments. Deadhead legs aren't segments.
    • §117.11 Table A: max flight time, 9:00 for reports 0500-1959, otherwise 8:00.
    • §117.25(e): 10 hours rest before any FDP or reserve, measured from release, with 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep opportunity.
    • §117.25(b): 30 consecutive hours free from all duty in the 168 hours before each FDP or reserve. Training counts as duty.
    • §117.23: 60 FDP hours in any 168, 190 FDP hours in any 672, 100 flight hours in any 672.
    • §117.27: no more than 3 FDPs in a row touching 0200-0559 (5 with a suitable-accommodation rest opportunity).
    • §117.25(g): a deadhead that exceeds Table B needs rest at least as long as the deadhead.
    • §117.21: short-call reserve availability period no longer than 14 hours.

    PSA/ALPA contract: flying

    • 12.A.1: scheduled duty no more than 13:00, or 14:00 for a continuous duty overnight. Actual duty can't be forced past 15:00 (12.A.2).
    • 12.A.4: no more than 5 consecutive duty days (6 in the blend).
    • 12.A.5 / 12.A.6: up to 4 sections per trip; no more than 3 CDOs in a row.
    • 12.B: 10 hours scheduled rest between duty periods, in or away from domicile.
    • 12.C: report 45 min before departure (30 if the section starts with a deadhead); release 15 min after block-in. Used when the schedule doesn't print report or release.
    • 12.D: 11 days off in a full month, with two blocks of 3 (or one of 6) and the rest in pairs.
    • 25.A.3: up to 7 legs per section, 3 per CDO. 25.A.6.a: lines up to 92 block hours. 25.A.6.c: a single CDO isn't followed by a section the same day.
    • 25.G.4: ready reserve up to 8 hours a day, 8 days a month.

    PSA/ALPA contract: training

    • 11.B.1: up to 6 consecutive training days, then a day off; no more than 50 training hours a week. Training schedules aren't subject to the Section 12 flying limits.
    • 11.B.2: no more than 10 hours of training a day. 11.B.4: no more than 4 sim hours a day.
    • 11.B.6: 9 hours rest before a checkride day (waivable).

    Assumptions

    • Unaugmented, two-pilot crew (CRJ). Acclimated, since all domestic flying stays within 60° of longitude.
    • Times on the schedule are local to each airport. Domicile time is used for calendar days and the 0200-0559 window.
    • Report and release come from the schedule's REPT and D-END lines when shown.
    • Split duty, augmented crews and extensions (§117.15, .17, .19) aren't modeled. Contract waivers aren't assumed.
    • Without carry-in data, time before the first duty on the schedule is treated as free.
    Planning aid only. Confirm legality with Crew Scheduling and your ALPA reps; the Captain confirms actual rest under CBA 12.B. Contract rules are from the PSA/ALPA agreement with its letters of agreement, as marked up (struck text excluded).
